PART I
Item 1. Business

Acme Robotics, Inc. ("Acme," "we," "us," or "the Company") designs, manufactures, and sells autonomous warehouse robotics systems to logistics and e-commerce customers across North America and Europe. We were incorporated in Delaware in 2011 and completed our initial public offering in 2019.

Our primary product line, the Sentinel series of autonomous mobile robots, is deployed in over 140 distribution centers operated by our customers as of the end of fiscal year 2023. We generate revenue primarily through hardware sales, recurring software subscription fees, and maintenance service contracts.

Item 1A. Risk Factors

Investing in our common stock involves a high degree of risk. You should carefully consider the risks described below, together with the other information in this Annual Report on Form 10-K, before making a decision to invest in our common stock. If any of the following risks actually occur, our business, financial condition, results of operations, and prospects could be materially and adversely affected.

We depend on a limited number of suppliers for critical robotics components, including lidar sensors and precision motors. If we are unable to obtain sufficient quantities of these components on commercially reasonable terms, or at all, our ability to manufacture and deliver our products on schedule would be materially and adversely affected.
